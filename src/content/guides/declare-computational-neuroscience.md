You must declare a concentration **no later than the middle of your fourth semester**.

## Five steps

1. **Review the requirements.** Read the [Computational Neuroscience ScB page in the University Bulletin](https://bulletin.brown.edu/the-college/concentrations/cneu/). Not every course is offered every semester, so plan ahead.
2. **Fill out the course plan worksheet** and send a copy to [neuroundergrad@brown.edu](mailto:neuroundergrad@brown.edu). You'll be assigned a concentration advisor.
3. **Meet your advisor** to talk through your course plan.
4. **Declare on ASK.** Go to **Degree Progress → Declaration**.
5. **Thinking about Honors?** Fill out the Honors application and email it to the Department of Neuroscience. Note the deadlines.
